# Keep Screen On

Uploading and downloading entries can take a while, especially when there are many\
entries, large media files, or a slow connection. If the screen turns off part-way\
through, the transfer can stop before it finishes — entries may be left part-uploaded\
or part-downloaded, and you may have to start again.

### What the setting does

While Epicollect5 is uploading or downloading entries, your screen stays on instead of turning off. As soon as the transfer finishes — whether it succeeds or fails — your screen goes back to behaving normally.

### Where to find it

Open **Settings** and turn on **Keep the screen on during uploads and downloads**.

### Good to know

* The setting is **off by default**. Nothing changes until you turn it on.
* It applies to both uploads and downloads.
* It is available in the Epicollect5 mobile app.
* Keeping the screen on uses more battery, so it is most useful when a transfer is large. You can switch it off again at any time.
* You must leave Epicollect5 open while the transfer runs — the screen will stay on. Pressing your device's power button will still turn the screen off as usual.

### When it is most useful

* Uploading a large number of entries, or entries with photos, audio or video.
* Downloading a project's remote entries for the first time.
* Working on a slow or unreliable connection, where transfers take longer.
